Lifestyle & Balance

Get Enough Sleep

Sleep is essential for focus and memory. Getting 7–8 hours of sleep helps you stay energized and perform better academically.

Manage Stress

College life can be stressful, but managing stress is important. Activities like exercise, relaxation, and socializing can help improve your well-being.

Stay Motivated

Setting clear goals and rewarding yourself helps maintain motivation. Remind yourself why you started and focus on progress.

Healthy Habits

Eating well, staying hydrated, and staying active improves both mental and physical health, helping you stay productive.

Balance Life

Make time for hobbies and relaxation. A balanced lifestyle prevents burnout and keeps you motivated long-term.
