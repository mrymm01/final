Study Tips

Stay Organized

Organization helps reduce stress and keeps you on track. Use planners, calendars, or apps to manage assignments and deadlines effectively. Staying organized also helps you feel more in control and less overwhelmed

Use Active Learning

Engage with your material by summarizing, using flashcards, or teaching others. Active learning improves understanding and memory. This method makes studying more effective compared to just reading notes.

Avoid Distractions

Limit phone use and study in a quiet environment. Even small distractions can reduce your focus significantly. Creating a focused environment can greatly improve your productivity.

Take Breaks

Short breaks help refresh your brain. Try studying for 25 minutes and resting for 5 minutes to stay productive. Taking breaks regularly helps you stay energized and prevent burnout.

Review Regularly

Instead of cramming, review your notes frequently. This improves long-term understanding and reduces exam stress. Consistent review builds confidence and improves exam performance.
