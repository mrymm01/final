Time Management

Create a Schedule

Planning your day helps you stay organized and reduces stress. Use a planner or digital calendar to map out your classes, assignments, and personal time.

Prioritize Tasks

Focus on what is most important first. Completing high-priority tasks early ensures that deadlines are met and prevents last-minute stress.

Avoid Procrastination

Break large tasks into smaller steps to make them easier to start. Taking action early helps you stay consistent and reduces pressure later.

Use Time Blocks

Allocate specific time periods for studying, relaxing, and other activities. This improves focus and helps maintain a balanced routine.

Stay Consistent

Following a routine daily helps build discipline. Consistency allows you to manage your time effectively and achieve long-term goals.
